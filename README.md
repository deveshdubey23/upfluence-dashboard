# 📈 Upfluence Influencer ROI Vetting Dashboard

A secure, high-performance web platform designed to systematically identify, audit, and rank high-value B2C creators across five target industries. This platform transforms superficial vanity metrics into a data-driven capital allocation engine, ensuring brands maximize their campaign efficiency while mitigating fraud risks.

## 🎯 Strategic Product Philosophy

### 1. The Core Engine: Upfluence Synergy Score (USS)

Instead of sorting creators by naive follower size, which is highly susceptible to bot inflation, the dashboard introduces a custom-weighted multi-criteria composite matrix called the **Upfluence Synergy Score (USS)**. It evaluates performance out of a maximum score of 100 based on four primary behavioral signals:

* **Audience Engagement (40% Weight):** Measures active community attention (likes, saves, comments) and acts as the leading predictor for campaign conversions.
* **Account Authenticity (30% Weight):** Functions as a programmatic fraud filter, heavily penalizing accounts displaying botted patterns or fake followers to protect brand budgets.
* **Post Frequency (20% Weight):** Measures content momentum (posts per week) to ensure algorithmic consistency and continuous audience touchpoints.
* **Gross Reach Footprint (10% Weight):** Total scale. Follower count is given the lowest weight because large distribution scale is only valuable if it passes the hurdles of authenticity and high engagement.

### 2. High-Conversion Industry Insights

Each specialized tab embeds industry-specific B2C data layers:

* **Beauty & Cosmetics:** Optimized for visual trust, cosmetic tutorial loops, and micro-advocate conversion tracking.
* **Fashion & Apparel:** Geared toward lookbook save frequency and multi-item styling asset cycles.
* **Fitness & Wellness:** Centered on scientific creator credibility, body transformations, and long-form workout vlogs.
* **Food & Culinary Arts:** Driven by satisfying recipe steps, texture close-ups, and pinned comment engagement.
* **Gaming & Esports:** Tailored for interactive broadcast streams, overlay integrations, and gameplay showcases.

## 💎 Premium UX Features

* **Multi-Creator Side-by-Side Comparison Modals:** Select two or more profiles simultaneously across tabs to display relative progress matchup bars and dynamically identify metric leaders (awarded with a 🏆).
* **Interactive Creator Deep-Dives:** Click any row to slide open a granular profile detailing biographies, recent sponsors, 30-day growth curves, audience demographics (age/gender splits), and direct booking reservation requests.
* **Programmatic Metric Tooltips:** Hovering over any performance attribute instantly displays its metric definition, marketing justification, industry benchmarks, and the creator's cohort percentile calculation.
* **Persistent Shortlists:** Built-in bookmarked "Saved Lists" safely preserve curated influencer shortlists directly inside local storage across page refreshes.
